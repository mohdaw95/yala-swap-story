The problem

Every home is full of things nobody uses.

A phone two upgrades old. A bike that fits nobody. The sofa from the last flat. Still worth something to someone, never worth the hassle of selling.

Selling is work

Pricing, haggling, no-shows, and strangers at the door. For most idle goods the cash is not worth the friction.

Classifieds assume cash

Every marketplace in the region is built around a price. If you would rather have a thing than the money, there is no place for you.

So it sits there

Cupboards fill, value rots, and the person two streets away who wanted exactly that never finds out.

The idea

If you have what I want and I have what you want, we don't need a price.

We need each other. Yala Swap makes that match the whole product: items on both sides, an optional cash top-up, and nothing else to argue about.

What protects it

A fee at the handshake only works if the handshake can't move off-platform.

Every marketplace that charges for contact leaks the same way: someone pastes a phone number into a message box. We designed that box out of existence.

Offers carry items, not words

An offer is a set of items and an optional amount. There is no free-text field to hide a number in, not in offers and not in counter-offers.

Questions are public and filtered

Questions on an ad are visible to everyone, so there is no private channel. Phone numbers and social handles are rejected in Arabic and English alike.

Enforced in the database

The rules live in the backend, not only in the interface. Bypassing the app does not bypass the rule.
